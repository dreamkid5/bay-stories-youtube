At the edge of a quiet village, an old lantern burned every night, though no one ever filled it with oil. One evening, a curious young traveler followed its golden light into the forest. There he found a lost child sheltering beneath a great baobab tree. He carried the child safely home, and the villagers finally understood the lantern's secret: it did not shine for itself. It appeared whenever someone needed the courage to help another person find their way home.
